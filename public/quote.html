About Our Estimates

Our rough estimates are calculated using average construction costs for the Springfield, Massachusetts area based on industry data from multiple sources including HomeBlue, Norman Builders, Manta, and regional construction cost studies for Western Massachusetts (2024-2025).*

Regional Cost Ranges (per square foot):

  • New Construction: $150-$270/sq ft
  • Full Renovation: $120-$300/sq ft
  • Home Addition: $150-$250/sq ft
  • Kitchen Remodel: $120-$225/sq ft
  • Bathroom Remodel: $120-$160/sq ft
  • Basement Finishing: $60-$150/sq ft
  • Deck/Patio: $30-$120/sq ft

*IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER: The estimates provided by this calculator are rough approximations based on regional average costs and are for informational purposes only. These estimates are NOT binding quotes and do not constitute a contract or commitment from Smith Brothers Construction. Actual project costs may vary significantly based on:

  • Specific site conditions and accessibility
  • Material selections and quality grades
  • Project complexity and custom requirements
  • Current market conditions and material availability
  • Permitting and regulatory requirements
  • Timeline and scheduling considerations

A detailed, binding quote will be provided after an in-person consultation and thorough project assessment. By submitting this form, you acknowledge that the rough estimate is not a guaranteed price and that final pricing will be determined through our formal quotation process.

Sources: Cost data compiled from HomeBlue.com, NormanBuilders.com, Manta.com, 3D Home Improvements 2025 Home Renovation Cost Guide for Western Massachusetts, and regional construction cost databases (2024-2025).
