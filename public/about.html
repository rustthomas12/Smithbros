About Smith Brothers

Building communities, one project at a time

Our Story

We are a group of brothers who have always shared the dream of owning a family business. In 2023, everything came together for us to be able to make that happen.

We provide a complete experience in building, repairing, and custom designing decks and porches. We take care of every aspect of the process from drawing the plans, submitting the permit, and setting up inspections, to delivering the exact exterior structure you were dreaming of. Every project is completely unique, and we pride ourselves on giving our customers exactly what they had in mind. All you have to do is describe what you would like and we'll take it from there. If you don't know what you want, we can help there as well with our creative, practical, and site specific solutions. We also offer staining/painting for pressure treated structures. We are available on an "as needed" basis, or we can set up a schedule for recuring staining.

Every project we undertake is treated with the same care and attention we'd give our own homes. From the initial consultation to the final walkthrough, we're committed to excellence at every stage.
